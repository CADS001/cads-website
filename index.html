The Challenge

Why Small Businesses
Miss Out on Funding

Despite available government and private funding, most small enterprises in South Africa fail to secure it — not because they lack potential, but preparation.

❌ Lack of compliance — CIPC, SARS, CSD registrations not in order

❌ Poor financial documentation — statements, projections not properly prepared

❌ Limited understanding — entrepreneurs unaware of funding requirements

❌ Weak business structures — plans and governance not investor-ready

The Ripple Effect

Funding institutions overwhelmed by unqualified applications

Entrepreneurs discouraged after repeated rejection cycles

Economic growth and job creation slowed across communities

CADS exists to break this cycle — one entrepreneur at a time.

Our Innovation

The CADS Two-Tier Model

We combine community-level reach with professional-grade support through two complementary arms that work in tandem.

Community Arm

CADS NPC

Non-Profit Community Outreach

  • Conducts outreach, workshops, and awareness campaigns
  • Screens and identifies potential entrepreneurs
  • Provides basic guidance and education
  • Builds trust and access in underserved communities
Commercial Arm

CADS Group Pty Ltd

Professional Funding Support Services

  • Conducts detailed funding readiness assessments
  • Supports compliance and documentation
  • Prepares and submits funding applications
  • Provides ongoing tracking and support

Implementation

Our 5-Step Pipeline

Every client moves through a structured, end-to-end process designed to maximise their chance of funding success.

01

Outreach & Awareness

Community workshops and campaigns that educate entrepreneurs on funding opportunities and requirements.

02

Screening & Qualification

Basic business readiness checks to identify viable candidates who are ready to move forward in the process.

03

Funding Readiness Assessment

In-depth evaluation of compliance status, financial documentation, and overall business viability.

04

Business Support Services

Hands-on support including compliance fixing, business documentation, and application preparation.

05

Funding Application & Support

Submission to relevant funders with ongoing tracking and support until a decision is reached.
